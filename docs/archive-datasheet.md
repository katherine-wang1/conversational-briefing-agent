## Known biases

- HN skews to developer interests; `hn_front_day` membership and rank are computed after the day (hindsight) and must not be used as a brief-time signal.
- HF daily-paper upvotes and HN Algolia points are as of the fetch date (labels only).
- `feed_pubdate` observations trust the feed's own dates; most feeds only keep their last 10-50 entries, so the window is covered only by feeds with long histories.
- `origin_live` bodies may be later revisions than what was visible at publish time.
- Wayback capture frequency varies by site and day; see the coverage table.
